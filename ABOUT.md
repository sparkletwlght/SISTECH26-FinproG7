# 📖 Project Brief & Overview: SISTECH 2026 Women Safety Platform

## 🎯 Collaborative Cross-Path Initiative
This project is part of the **SISTECH 2026 Final Project**, a collaborative cross-path initiative where participants across different tracks—**UI/UX, Front-End Engineering, Machine Learning and Operations (MLOps), Digital Marketing, Product Management, and Business Analysis & Strategy**—work together to develop a comprehensive web-based women safety platform. 

The platform integrates crowdsourced inputs, location intelligence, and predictive analytics to help users plan safer commutes, report incidents securely, and access emergency support quickly.

---

## 🛑 Problem Statement
Public safety in urban environments faces critical challenges:
- **Harassment in public spaces:** Posing constant risks to individuals during daily routines.
- **Unsafe commutes:** Especially pronounced during night hours or in unfamiliar areas due to poor lighting and isolated pathways.
- **Underreported cases:** Driven by fear, social stigma, or a lack of trust in traditional reporting systems.

---

## 🛠️ Core Product & Scope
The platform provides three primary pillars:
1. **Safe Route Recommendation & Risk Prediction:** AI/ML-backed route assessment.
2. **Anonymous Reporting:** Secure, privacy-aware incident logging.
3. **Emergency Feature:** Rapid-access SOS and safe point navigation.

### Frontend Implementation Requirements
For this final project scope, the front-end development includes:
- Building the frontend web application based on the UI/UX prototype.
- **Consuming the MLOps Risk Prediction API** and visualizing outputs clearly (frontend strictly consumes the API with **no ML logic** executed on the client side).
- Building an interactive safety heatmap using a mapping library (e.g., Leaflet/Mapbox) with **time-based filters (hour/day)** to dynamically update heatmap layers and risk insights.
- Implementing the **“Safe Commute Insights” UI** where users select a start point and destination to view risk predictions and safety analysis for specific time windows.
- Implementing the **Anonymous Reporting UI** with secure and privacy-aware form handling (mocked or endpoint-connected).
- Implementing the **Emergency Feature UI** (SOS screen, trusted contacts mock, and static nearest safe points view).
- Ensuring full responsiveness and performance across both desktop and mobile viewports.
- Deploying the frontend web application for final demo and presentation.
- Integrating **one extra feature** determined by the group.
